# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want the Prisma CLI community to be welcoming, respectful, and productive for
everyone who participates in good faith.

We expect contributors, maintainers, and community members to treat each other
with care, assume good intent where reasonable, and keep discussions focused on
improving the project.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful of different viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, docs, and behavior, not on people.
- Take responsibility when your words or actions cause harm.
- Help keep public project spaces useful, welcoming, and on topic.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, or sustained unwanted attention.
- Insults, personal attacks, or demeaning comments.
- Discrimination or stereotyping based on identity or personal traits.
- Sexualized language or attention in project spaces.
- Sharing private information without permission.
- Threats, encouragement of violence, or other behavior that endangers people.
- Spam, impersonation, or intentionally misleading participation.

## Reporting

If you need to report a possible Code of Conduct violation, please use Prisma's
Discord community at <https://pris.ly/discord>.

You can send a private message to a server moderator or use the private
moderation flow if one is available. Reports will be handled with care for
safety, privacy, and confidentiality.

Do not use public GitHub issues for private conduct reports.

## Enforcement

Maintainers and moderators may take action that they judge appropriate,
including warnings, temporary limits on participation, or removal from project
spaces. Enforcement decisions are made with the goals of protecting the
community, repairing harm when possible, and keeping project spaces healthy.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in Prisma CLI project spaces and when someone is
officially representing the project in public.
